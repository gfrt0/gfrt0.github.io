### [europolls](https://gfrt0.github.io/europolls/)

Wikipedia-direct dataset of national voting-intention polls for 32 European
countries (EU-27 plus the UK, Norway, Iceland, and Switzerland). Roughly 33k
polls and 296k party-poll observations from 2002 onward, with party-name
harmonization via Party Facts IDs. The browser front-end fetches and parses
Wikipedia HTML client-side; no backend.

[live site](https://gfrt0.github.io/europolls/) &middot;
[repo](https://github.com/gfrt0/europolls)

### [spese fiscali](https://gfrt0.github.io/spesefiscali/)

Searchable, filterable cross-edition database of Italian *spese fiscali* (tax
expenditures). Covers Tavola 1 of every *Rapporto annuale sulle spese fiscali*
from RSF 2016 through RSF 2024 — roughly five thousand *spese fiscali erariali*
across nine editions, with a stable cross-edition ID so the same measure can be
tracked over time. Each row carries identifying number, governing norm with
Normattiva deep-link, description, tax and fiscal category, nature, sunset date,
three-year fiscal impact, beneficiaries, source-PDF page link, and government
attributions (introducing executive and executive at most recent amendment).

[live site](https://gfrt0.github.io/spesefiscali/) &middot;
[repo](https://github.com/gfrt0/spesefiscali)
