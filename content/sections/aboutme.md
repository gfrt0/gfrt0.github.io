I am a PhD candidate in economics at [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/economics/) and
[cemmap](https://www.cemmap.ac.uk/).

I study how information and policy shape equilibrium outcomes in oligopolistic
markets, particularly digital, energy, and environmental ones.\
I also develop estimation methods for the dynamic competition questions common
to these markets.

**I am on the job market during the 2026-27 academic year.**
